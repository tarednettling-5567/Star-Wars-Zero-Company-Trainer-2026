## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **STAR WARS Zero Company™**. This trainer enhances the tactical campaign by providing control over squad survivability, Action Points, Advantage, and mission progression. It is designed around the game's turn-based combat, cover system, squad abilities, bonds, and strategic operations.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more forgiving campaign, faster squad development, easier experimentation with operator abilities, or additional control over challenging turn-based missions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Movement takes place on tactical maps where each operator spends Action Points to reach positions, take cover, reposition, or assist allies. Use the Action Point option to cross dangerous areas, secure stronger cover, and adjust the squad's formation without being limited by the normal turn economy.

### Tools & Inventory

Operators can be customized through their combat specializations, appearances, loadouts, and abilities. Use the trainer to make experimentation safer when testing different squad roles and equipment combinations between missions.

### World & Environment

The campaign combines tactical missions with strategic Operations and downtime at the squad's base. Progress through the Clone Wars story, manage the team, develop bonds between squad members, and deploy the most suitable operators for each objective.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is turn-based and rewards cover, line of sight, elevation, flanking, overwatch, and coordinated squad actions. Each operator normally receives three Action Points per turn, while Advantage enables stronger tactical plays. The trainer's health, Action Point, Advantage, revive, and ability options can be combined to explore battles without the usual penalties.
